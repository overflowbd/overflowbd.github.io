Privacy Policy for KeyZoo

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

Thank you for using KeyZoo. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting users' information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the KeyZoo mobile application.

About KeyZoo

KeyZoo is a children's mobile game inspired by piano-style tapping gameplay. The application can be used without creating an account, logging in, or granting special device permissions.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or process personal information from users.

KeyZoo does not require:

  • User registration
  • User login
  • Name collection
  • Email collection
  • Phone number collection
  • Location access
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Contacts access

Advertising

KeyZoo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information to provide, improve, and measure advertising services. Such information may include:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • App interaction data
  • Diagnostics and performance information

The collection and use of this information are governed by Google's privacy policies.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Information

Children's Privacy

KeyZoo is intended for children and families.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any information collected through advertising services is handled by Google AdMob according to Google's privacy practices and applicable laws.

Where applicable, we configure advertising settings to support compliance with Google Play Families policies and child-directed treatment settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Any information automatically collected by Google AdMob is managed according to Google's own policies and practices.

Data Security

Since we do not directly collect personal information, we do not maintain user databases containing personal data. We take reasonable measures to keep the application secure and updated.
